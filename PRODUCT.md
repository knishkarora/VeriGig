# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Campus Students:** Undergraduate and postgraduate students (e.g., PCTE, GNDEC, Thapar) seeking verified, flexible micro-gigs (₹2,500 - ₹25,000) that build verified on-platform portfolio experience without scam risk or delayed payments.
2. **Local SMEs & Startups:** Regional business owners, clinic directors, retail cafes, and early-stage founders needing fast, high-quality execution on discreet digital deliverables (Figma UI, reels, automation, landing pages) with milestone-backed escrow protection.
3. **Campus Placement & Governance Admins:** University administrators and platform governance staff who verify institutional student identity, review dispute claims, and audit talent engagement.

## Product Purpose

CampusGigs (VeriGig) is a localized micro-gig marketplace connecting university students with verified local businesses for milestone-backed, escrow-secured digital projects. Success means students earn real income with zero payout friction, while local SMEs receive professional deliverables with instant accountability.

## Positioning

Unlike open, global freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr) plagued by bidding wars, overseas spam, and delayed payouts, VeriGig enforces:
- Institutional student verification (Roll numbers, college credentials, department tags).
- Strict milestone-gated escrow where funds are pre-locked before work commences.
- Hyperlocal credibility (pairing students with regional businesses and alumni founders).

## Operating Context

- Students access the platform between classes and study sessions on laptops and mobile devices.
- Employers manage jobs from office desktops or phones, reviewing portfolios and issuing quick sign-offs.
- Escrow milestones are funded upfront and released upon milestone sign-off.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:** Role-switching simulation (Student, Employer, Admin), micro-gig explorer with multi-faceted filtering, step-by-step gig creation wizard with milestone breakdown, interactive proposal submissions, 4-stage deliverable inspection workspace, ID verification queue, and dispute resolution arbitration.
- **Constraints:** Frontend-driven demonstration prototype running React 19, TypeScript, and Vite with Tailwind CSS and reactive in-memory context state (`MarketplaceContext`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** CampusGigs (VeriGig)
- **Voice:** Energetic, precise, institutional yet youthful, editorial, uncompromising on craft.
- **Visual anchors:** High-contrast jet-black cards (`#121214`), soft lavender/canvas background (`#F5F2FD`), acid-lime accent highlights (`#D4F851`), tactile rounded surfaces, and clear typography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Authentic Punjab/North India university seed data (PCTE Group of Institutes, GNDEC, Thapar Institute).
- Real local SME scenarios (Bakeology Cafe, Apex Dental Clinic, Ludhiana Cycles, Silkberry Apparel).

## Product Principles

1. **Escrow Transparency:** Every rupee locked in escrow is visible, attributed to a specific milestone deliverable, and released with one clear action.
2. **Institutional Dignity:** Student profiles display verified college credentials, roll IDs, and skill endorsements—elevating micro-gigs into real resume capital.
3. **Tactile Clarity:** Every state transition (proposal sent, milestone active, work submitted, sign-off complete) has a clear visual status and instant confirmation.
